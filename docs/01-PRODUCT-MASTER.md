# Find Playpal — Product Master

**Status:** Early Draft  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Define the core product direction of Find Playpal and serve as the foundation for future product, UI/UX, development, and AI-assisted design decisions.

---

# 1. Product Overview

## Product Name

**Find Playpal**

## Product Type

Find Playpal is a social entertainment and game companion platform that connects people through games, shared interests, interaction, and paid companionship services.

The platform is designed to combine:

- Game companion services
- Social discovery
- Interest-based connections
- Personal expression
- Community interaction
- Virtual identity and virtual goods

Find Playpal should not be treated only as a transaction platform.

The long-term product direction is to create a place where users can **play, meet, interact, express themselves, and build connections**.

---

# 2. Core Product Purpose

Find Playpal aims to provide users with several core experiences.

## 2.1 Find Someone to Play With

Users can discover and pay other users or game companions to play games together.

The platform should make the process of:

**Discover → Choose → Order → Play → Interact**

simple and approachable.

---

## 2.2 Meet New People

Find Playpal also acts as a social discovery platform.

Users should be able to meet people beyond their existing social circle and form new connections through:

- Games
- Shared interests
- Content
- Communities
- Activities
- Social interaction

The platform should reduce the friction normally involved in meeting new people.

---

## 2.3 Find People With Shared Interests

Connections should not depend only on appearance or popularity.

Users should also be able to discover people through common interests such as:

- Games
- Entertainment
- Hobbies
- Lifestyle
- Communities
- Other interests

**Detailed interest taxonomy: TBD**

---

## 2.4 Personal Expression

Find Playpal should give users room to express their personality and identity.

This may include:

- Profile customization
- User-generated content
- Virtual avatars
- Decorations
- Achievements
- Collections
- Social status
- Other personalization systems

The objective is to allow users to feel that their profile and presence on the platform belong to them.

---

## 2.5 Sense of Belonging

Some users may not only be looking for individual interactions.

They may also be looking for:

- Recognition
- Familiar people
- Communities
- Social presence
- Shared identity
- A place where they feel noticed or included

Find Playpal should therefore consider **belonging and community** as part of its long-term product experience.

---

# 3. Core Product Pillars

The current product can be summarized into five core pillars.

## Pillar 1 — Play

Help users find people to play games with.

## Pillar 2 — Connect

Help users meet and interact with new people.

## Pillar 3 — Discover

Help users discover people who share similar games, interests, or personalities.

## Pillar 4 — Express

Give users ways to express their identity, personality, achievements, and virtual presence.

## Pillar 5 — Belong

Create social experiences that encourage users to return, participate, and become part of communities.

These pillars should later be used when evaluating new features.

A feature should ideally strengthen at least one of these pillars.

# 4. Target Users

The following target audiences are currently identified.

## 4.1 Gamers

People who regularly play games and are interested in finding other people to play with.

---

## 4.2 Users Looking for Game Companions

Users who specifically want someone to accompany them while gaming and are willing to pay for that service.

---

## 4.3 Social Discovery Users

People who want to:

- Meet new people
- Make friends
- Find people with similar interests
- Expand their social circle

---

## 4.4 Interest-Based Social Users

Users who prefer connecting through shared interests instead of traditional social networking.

---

## 4.5 Appearance-Led Discovery Users

Some users may also be interested in discovering and interacting with people they find attractive.

This may contribute to social discovery, but it should not become the only discovery mechanism of the platform.

---

## 4.6 Belonging-Seeking Users

People who want a social platform where they can participate, interact, and feel part of a group or community.

---

## 4.7 Status and Recognition Users

Some users enjoy:

- Displaying achievements
- Showing collections
- Showing virtual goods
- Demonstrating social status
- Receiving attention
- Receiving recognition from others

The platform may support this through appropriate social and virtual-economy systems.

---

## 4.8 Virtual Identity Users

Users who enjoy:

- Avatars
- Virtual characters
- Profile customization
- Digital collectibles
- Decorations
- Personal virtual identities

# 26. AI Design Rule

AI design assistants must not assume that every existing Figma screen or previous prototype represents the current product standard.

Before modifying a page, the AI should check:

1. Product Master
2. Design Foundation
3. Component System
4. Page Specification
5. Design Decisions
6. Existing approved implementation

When conflicts exist, the AI should identify the conflict instead of silently deciding which version is correct.

# 27. Current Product Statement

**Find Playpal is a social entertainment and game companion platform designed to help people play together, discover new connections, express themselves, and participate in a shared social environment.**

Paid companionship is an important part of the business model, but the platform's broader direction also includes social discovery, identity, community, and virtual experiences.
